Request a Feature Story

We would love to hear from anyone running an inn in Chiba Prefecture.
Coverage, photography, and writing — even help getting your website and social media in shape — are all free of charge.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Send us the form below
  • We reply within 3 business days
  • We talk it over — online or at your inn
  • We visit for the story, photos, and writing
  • Your feature goes live, shared on social media too

Not comfortable with self-promotion? That’s fine.
Just get in touch and we will come to you.

Prefer to talk by phone?
If it’s urgent, include your phone number in the form and we will call you back.

You can reach us through a Google Form. It takes about a minute, and we reply within 3 business days. The form is in Japanese, but please feel free to write in English.
