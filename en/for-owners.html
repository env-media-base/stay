SERVICE

For the day you might one day sell —
start growing your inn's value today.

Through our coverage of inns across Chiba, Kakureyado has listened closely to what owners hope for.
Again and again, we hear the same thing from owners in their 50s and 60s:
"Retirement is still a way off, but I want to hand this place on in the best shape I can."
We want to be the partner who stands beside that forward-looking wish and grows an inn's value together.

Does this sound familiar?

"I want to do what I can, while I still can."

You haven't decided on a successor yet. But if the day ever comes to pass the inn on or sell it, the better its condition, the more options you will have. The state of the building, your reputation with guests, how the inn appears online — putting these in order now can make a real difference to your inn's value a few years down the road.

How Kakureyado can help

An inn guests want to return to — seen through younger eyes.

We are a web magazine that has walked Chiba and visited its inns in person. We know, first-hand, the atmosphere that makes today's younger travelers say "I want to stay there" — how photos should look, how information should be shared. We use that perspective to draw out your inn's charm and shape it so it reaches the next generation of guests.

WHO & WHY

Who we are —
and why it's free.

We know "free" can sound too good to be true.
So here, honestly, is who runs this magazine and why we charge nothing.

Editor

Yuta Tsuchiya (Tsuchiya Yuta)

A Chiba-based freelancer working in corporate sustainability sales and community management. Before going independent, I served individual and corporate clients at a regional bank. Driven by a wish to pass Chiba's good places on to the next generation, I now visit the prefecture's inns one by one.

Why is it free?

Because one day, we hope to run an inn in Chiba ourselves.

To be completely honest: we hope to open an experience-focused inn in Chiba someday. That is why, right now, we want to learn from the innkeepers who came before us and build relationships that last — and it is why our coverage and support are free. If that someday leads to talk of succession or partnership, all the better for both of us. We would rather tell you that openly, self-interest and all. Which is also why there is no sales pitch, and no hidden fees billed later.

ABOUT US

Connections with inns,
built one visit at a time.

Kakureyado visits Chiba's inns one by one — places you won't find on the major booking sites —
and writes every article from conversations with the owners themselves.
That accumulated groundwork is our strength.

01

A deep archive of first-hand coverage

Minshuku (family-run guesthouses), kominka (old folk houses), whole-house rentals, mountain lodges, machiya townhouses — we have visited and covered inns of every kind across Chiba, telling each owner's story and craft with care. We bring that storytelling know-how to sharing your inn's charm.

02

Contact with the front line of inn-making

Through our reporting, we also keep up ongoing exchanges with owners of properties leading the way in design and digital communication. From those latest practices, we propose concrete ideas that could work just as well for inns in Chiba.

03

A younger generation's instincts, applied to your inn

Our team belongs to the generation that actually travels and chooses these inns. How photos are taken, how an inn appears on social media, how easy it is to book — we can tell you first-hand what younger guests look at when they choose where to stay.

04

Consultation and coverage, free of charge

Interviews, photography, writing, and basic help setting up your website and social media are all provided at no cost. "I just want to talk it through first" is a perfectly good place to start. Please reach out without worrying about fees.
