ABOUT US

Inns you don't just stay at —
inns you meet.

Kakureyado visits inns and cafés across Japan that never appear on the major booking sites,
and quietly shares them — owners, stories and all.

01

Not on any
booking site

We only feature inns you can't reach through the big booking platforms — small, local places tucked away across Japan that the algorithms never surface.

02

Experiences you'll find
nowhere else

The people who kept these buildings alive, and the life of the land around them. One story at a time. Not travel to consume — time you'll remember.

03

Before they
disappear

Many of these inns face closure — no successor, too few guests. Before they're gone, we want you to see the view from their windows and feel the warmth of the hands that keep them.
