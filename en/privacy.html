Privacy Policy

Established and last updated: January 2026

This English translation is provided for convenience. The Japanese version prevails.
Kakureyado ("this magazine") regards the protection of your personal information as an important responsibility and is committed to managing and handling it appropriately. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we manage it.

1. Information We Collect

We may receive personal information from you in the following situations:

  • Information entered in the coverage and feature inquiry form (inn name, location, your name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • The email address you enter when signing up for our newsletter
  • Information you provide when contacting us

In addition, when you access this website, information such as your IP address, browser type, and access date and time may be recorded automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide only for the following purposes:

  • Contacting and coordinating with you regarding coverage and features
  • Sending our newsletter
  • Responding to inquiries
  • Statistical analysis to improve our services (in a form that does not identify individuals)

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

We will not disclose or provide your personal information to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • When we have your consent
  • When disclosure is required by law
  • When necessary to protect a person's life, physical safety, or property, and obtaining your consent is difficult

4. Security

We take appropriate security measures to prevent unauthorized access to, and the loss, damage, alteration, or leakage of, personal information. Access to personal information is limited to the minimum number of people necessary, and it is managed with care.

5. Unsubscribing from the Newsletter

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, either via the unsubscribe link in any email we send or by contacting us at the address below. We will process your request within five business days of receiving it.

6. Use of Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve usability and analyze site traffic. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly if you do so.

7. Analytics Tools

We may use analytics tools to improve our services. These tools collect traffic data, but they do not identify individuals. The data collected is managed in accordance with the privacy policy of each tool provider.

8. Access, Correction, and Deletion of Personal Information

If you would like to access, correct, or delete your personal information, please contact us at the address below. After verifying your identity, we will respond promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised without notice in response to changes in the law or to our services. The revised policy takes effect at the time it is posted on this page.

Contact

For questions or requests regarding the handling of personal information, please reach us via the contact form.
We will reply within three business days.
